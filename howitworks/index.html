How it works

Universe Courier is a small experiment in chance and meaning. Nobody picks the messages. The timing and the choice both come from radioactive decay, which is about as random as nature gets.

  1. Listen. A true random number generator produces a steady stream of numbers from 1 to 128.
  2. Wait for a sign. Every member is given two key pairs of their own, chosen at random and never shared with anyone else. When one of your pairs appears back to back in the stream (for example 3 then 10, or 43 then 2), a message is on its way to you.
  3. Choose. The three numbers just before that pair pick the message: a tarot card, an I Ching hexagram, a piece of guidance, a question to sit with, an affirmation, a mystery, an answer, or one of your own messages. You decide which kinds you get, and how often, in your settings.
  4. Deliver. The message reaches you however you choose: push notification, email, or text message. Set quiet hours and anything that arrives overnight waits until morning.

An example from the stream, newest number first:

243415387

The gold pair (43, then 2) is one member’s key pair, so it sends them a message. The three numbers before it (87, 53, 41) decide which one.

What to make of a message

Most messages will just be messages. Every so often, one will land on a day when it means something, and you'll notice. Nothing here predicts the future or claims a hidden hand behind it. Universe Courier simply leaves the door open. What comes through is up to you.
