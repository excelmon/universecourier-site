    The numbers come from a hardware random number generator driven by radioactive decay, read by a Raspberry Pi. When two numbers land in one of the key pairs, three more numbers pick a message and it's sent as a push notification.

    This page refreshes itself every minute; the Pi publishes new data every 5 minutes.
